Returns energy and force between bond due to state-based model.

Linear peridynamic solid in dimension d (Silling 2010 for 3D; Yang et al. 2024, JMPS 191, eqs. 5-11 for 2D): \( t = J [ r \theta (d \kappa / m - \alpha / d) + \alpha e ] \) with \( \alpha = d(d+2) G / m \) and \( e^d = e - \theta r / d \). The returned energy is the deviatoric part at x, \( \frac{\alpha}{2} J (e^d)^2 \); the dilatational part is getDilatationEnergyDensity().
