Returns energy and force between bond due to pairwise interaction.

Micropotential \( w = \frac{1}{2} c s^2 r \) and pair force \( c s \) (Silling and Askari 2005), both times \( J(r/\delta) \). The returned energy is the bond's share of the strain energy density at x, \( W(x) = \frac{1}{2} \int w \, dy \), i.e. \( w/2 \).
