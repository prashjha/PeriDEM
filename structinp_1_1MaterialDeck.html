Returns the block with the given fields set

The extent of a bond is given either as Horizon, a length, or as Horizon_Mesh_Ratio, a multiple of the mesh size; naming one of the two is what selects it. The elastic constants K, G and Gc are written as given, since a material that states none of them cannot be built, and E is derived from K when it is not named.
